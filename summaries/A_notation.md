This appendix gathers the notation of the book and its link to the code. It lists the symbols used in the chapters (effects, priors, posteriors, thresholds, error rates, information and sample sizes), the correspondence between the notation and the arguments of the functions in the bayestrials package (such as the one sided type I error alpha, an effect theta, response rates p0 and p1, the number of patients or events n, and the number of simulated trials nsim), a list of abbreviations (for example FDA, EMA, ICH, DMC, SAP, MID, ESS, PP, FWER, FDR and MCP Mod), and a list of key terms from the whole book. It is a reference for readers who meet a symbol out of its chapter and want its meaning, its usual scale and the name of the matching function argument. It is also a place to look up an abbreviation before reading the chapter that defines it. The list of key terms at the end collects the vocabulary a reader would search for. Readers who meet a symbol out of its chapter can start here. It contains no results.

## From the literature

None. The symbols follow the usual statistical conventions of the cited literature on Bayesian trials, group sequential designs and e-values.
