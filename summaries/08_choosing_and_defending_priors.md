# Chapter 8. Choosing and Defending Priors

This chapter treats the prior. It begins with the fact that the traditional design has no stated prior but does carry an implicit one, describes where priors come from in a sponsor's program (including the phase 2 selection problem), and gives skeptical, enthusiastic and noninformative priors. It shows why default flat priors fail on the scale that matters, through independent beta priors on the risk difference and flat priors on logistic coefficients, and introduces the conditional means prior. It measures how much a prior weighs by effective sample size, treats prior data conflict and robust priors, and gives sensitivity analysis over the prior scale, including the tipping point prior. It then reads what the FDA draft says, including the device precedent, compares the protocol side by side with the traditional one, shows how to write the prior into the protocol and what can go wrong. Cases are pediatric belimumab in lupus, REMAP-CAP and the multiplatform heparin trials, EOLIA and BNT162b2.
