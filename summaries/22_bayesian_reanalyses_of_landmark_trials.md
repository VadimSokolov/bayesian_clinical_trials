# Chapter 22. Bayesian Reanalyses of Landmark Trials

This chapter reanalyzes published trials in Bayesian terms from their reported estimates. It starts with the normal approximation and its posterior, then GUSTO (when prior trials disagree with the new one), EOLIA (probable benefit after a nonsignificant primary analysis), ANDROMEDA SHOCK (the same pattern with a hazard ratio) and RECOVERY (subgroups and shrinkage). It states what the four reanalyses share and what a reanalysis can and cannot claim: prespecification, prior choice, the regulator's view, and why a reanalysis is not a new trial. A section on what critics say places the cases in the literature on reanalysis and sets the critique of de Grooth and Cremer (that many priors do not contribute to consensus) beside the skeptical prior as an adversary. It ends with exercises.

## From the literature
